# Code of Conduct / 行为规范

## English

Wrist Remote contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration safe, respectful, and evidence-based.

### Expected behavior

- Discuss code and behavior without attacking people.
- Give reproducible evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and distinguish automated checks from real-device acceptance.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's identifier, credential, path, transcript, recording, screenshot, or device information.
- Credit sources and preserve license and attribution requirements.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, security, compatibility, and release readiness.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private communications or personal data without permission.
- Pressuring anyone to share credentials, certificates, devices, accounts, or production access.
- Knowingly bypassing security checks, concealing test failures, or presenting unverified behavior as accepted.
- Using project channels for spam, unrelated promotion, or coordinated disruption.

### Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private reporting channel. Do not include unrelated personal data. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, close contributions, or ban accounts according to severity and recurrence. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and shared only with people who need to act on them.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. This policy does not require agreement; it requires professional conduct.

## 简体中文

Wrist Remote 贡献者应当保持安全、尊重且以证据为依据的技术协作。

### 应当做到

- 讨论代码和行为，不攻击个人。
- 提供可复现证据，承认不确定性，区分自动化检查与真机验收。
- 尊重隐私，不公开他人的标识、凭据、路径、转写、录音、截图或设备信息。
- 标明来源并遵守许可证和归属要求。
- 尊重维护者对范围、安全、兼容和发布条件的决定。

### 不可接受

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化表达或持续的人身攻击。
- 未经允许公开私密通信或个人资料。
- 施压要求他人提供凭据、证书、设备、账号或生产访问权。
- 明知故犯地绕过安全检查、隐瞒测试失败，或把未验证行为描述为已验收。
- 在项目渠道发布垃圾信息、无关推广或组织干扰。

### 执行

通过仓库的私有报告渠道提交行为问题，不附带无关个人资料。维护者可根据严重程度和重复情况编辑或删除内容、限制参与、关闭贡献或封禁账号。执行记录应保留在私有渠道，只向需要采取行动的人披露。

善意技术分歧是正常的。本规范不要求所有人意见一致，只要求保持专业行为。
